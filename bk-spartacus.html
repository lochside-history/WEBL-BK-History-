🏆 LOCHSIDE LEGACY

BK Spartacus proved that size means nothing next to heart and will, forging one of the toughest careers in Lochside history.

Standing at just 5 foot 8 inches, he defied every odd to claim 89 regional titles and stand tall among the division’s greatest names.

His relentless style and granite chin made him a fan favourite, battling and beating much larger opponents across his legendary run.

His 22-fight win streak remains one of the most inspiring achievements in the gym’s record books.
