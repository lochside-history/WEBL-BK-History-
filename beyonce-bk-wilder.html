🏆 LOCHSIDE LEGACY

Beyonce BK Wilder built one of the most remarkable heavyweight careers in the Lochside record books.

Standing at 7 feet 1 inch, Wilder combined extraordinary longevity with championship success, recording 107 regional title wins across his career.

His career featured repeated battles against major heavyweight names, including Vitali "Doctor Ironfist" Klitschko, Graf Diacula, Border Control, Chris "La-la-la-la" de Burgh, Big Bonned Benny Bruise and Brutal Tribute.

His 24-fight winning streak stands as one of the defining achievements of his Lochside career.
